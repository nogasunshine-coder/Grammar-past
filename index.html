Past Simple vs

📖 Theory

Past Simple

Used for actions that started and finished in the past. The action is complete — it's over.

Subject + V₂ (or didn't + V₁)
She called him yesterday.

⏱ Time expressions:

yesterdaylast nightlast yearagoin 2005oncewhen I was youngfinally

Past Continuous

Used when:
1. Two actions happen at the same time in the past
2. A longer action is interrupted by a shorter one
3. Setting the scene / background in a story

Subject + was/were + V-ing
It was raining when I left.

⏱ Time expressions:

whilewhenat that momentat 8 o'clockall dayasat the time
